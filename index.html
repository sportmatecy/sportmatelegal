Privacy Policy

SportMate CY is an app for finding partners for sports and social activities, and specialist services, in Cyprus. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name, phone number or email (at sign-up)Creating and signing in to your account
Profile photoShown to other users in listings and chats
City and the map point you set for a listingShowing your listing to other users and building directions
Listing text, responses, chat messagesThe app's core function — finding partners and communicating
Ratings and reviews about meetupsTrust between users
Device push tokenNotifications about new responses, messages and reminders
Interface languageShowing the app in your chosen language

We do not process payments inside the app. The price shown on a paid listing is informational; payment for a service happens directly between you and the specialist, outside the app.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase — stores account data, listings, messages and files (database and cloud storage).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Google Places / Maps — address lookup and map display when picking a meeting place.

We do not sell personal data to third parties and do not use it for ad targeting.

How long we keep data

Data is kept for as long as you have an account. Messages and completed listings move to an archive automatically but are not deleted automatically. When you delete your account (see below), we remove or anonymize the data tied to you.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time directly in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently disables sign-in to that account. Your profile is anonymized, your photos are removed, and any of your pending (unaccepted) join requests are deleted. Shared history that involves other people — a completed meetup, a chat, a review — is kept for their record, shown under an anonymous "Deleted user" label instead of your name.

If you'd like a copy of your data before deleting it, write to the contact email below and we'll send it to you.

Your rights

  • View and edit your profile — right in the app.
  • Request an export of your data — by email below.
  • Turn off specific notification types — in profile settings.
  • Delete your account at any time — in the app, as described above.

If you're in the EU, you have rights under GDPR, including the right to access, correct, delete and port your data.

Children

SportMate CY is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from users under that age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll notify users inside the app. The date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.

Privacy questions and data export requests: sportmatecy@gmail.com

Reviews and ratings
